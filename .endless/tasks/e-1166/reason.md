E-1166 identified that _default_base_branch in src/endless/worktree_cmd.py falls back to literal 'main' when 'git symbolic-ref refs/remotes/origin/HEAD' is unset (fresh clones), which is wrong for master/develop repos. E-1940 hit the same defect on the Go side — the unsettled probe and the worktree reaper both hardcode 'main' in 'git rev-list main..HEAD' — and its plan specifies ONE shared resolution order (.endless/config.json key, then origin/HEAD, then init.defaultBranch, then probe main/master, then a real error rather than a silent fallback) applied to all three call sites, Go and Python. E-1166's proposed fallbacks (init.defaultBranch, ls-remote --symref) are preserved as steps in that order. Splitting the two would have meant two resolvers that could disagree about the same repo.
