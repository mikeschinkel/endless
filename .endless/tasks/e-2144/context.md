Endless glosses the obsolete status as 'Made irrelevant by other changes -- it never needed doing', and every transition into it carries the label 'retires -- it never needed doing' in the Go transition table.

That is not what the word means.

Cambridge: 'no longer in use, out of date, or replaced by something newer and better.'

The gloss excludes the commonest real case -- work that was worth doing when filed and has since been superseded -- and pushes it toward declined, which means an active decision not to do something and is a different fact.

Observed 2026-09-14: this wording led an agent to question a correct retirement of E-1421, E-1441, E-1442 and E-746 after session status and session monitor superseded them.
