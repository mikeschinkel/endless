E-1920 shipped `decision supersede` with no way to say WHY a decision stopped governing, on the reasoning that the successor is the reason.

That fails whenever the cause is a fact about the world rather than about the successor:

ED-1067 was superseded by ED-1570 because both of ED-1067's consumers died, which ED-1570 does not say.

`unreject` clears rejection_reason (E-1864) and `reinstate` clears obsolete_reason (E-1920, copied from it), silently and unrecoverably.
