Fires only on a self-dev land of Endless itself, and only when the landed branch changed Python the landing process loads after the merge. Documented twice: 2026-05-29 landing E-1510, and 2026-08-20 landing E-2011, where renaming project_path.normalize to resolved killed the land with 'cannot import name resolved'. Both were recovered by re-running the land, and the landing was recorded on the retry.

Measured 2026-09-30, and it narrows the problem sharply: 760 landings are on record, and no code-bearing task has been missing a landing row since May 2026 (88 of the gaps are April, 2 are May, 0 since; the rest are research and brainstorm tasks, which have branches but no code to land). So this does NOT cost landing records, and task_landings is reliable for the current era — an earlier reading of task unlanded's totals as a reliability signal was wrong, and the April backlog it was counting pre-dates the recording mechanism.

What it does cost is an occasional land that dies partway with a confusing ImportError and needs a manual re-run, twice in five months. The unquantified part is the silent variant: a module imported for the first time after the merge reading an already-cached module's changed constant, which raises nothing. That has never been observed and by construction would leave no trace.
