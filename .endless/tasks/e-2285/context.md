Today setup problems only surface after something fails, spread across errors list, worktree check and jobs list. Design from the doctor brainstorm: doctor reports problems only, ordered by severity then scope, and points to --suggestions, which lists unset opt-in settings with reasons from the settings registry. Errors and job health are folded in. --accept <check-id> --reason records a problem the user chooses to live with, at the check's scope and bound to the observed condition; accepted problems are counted and listed with --accepted. --fix has three tiers (auto, confirm, advice only) and is user-only. --all-projects runs per-project checks in every registered project plus cross-project checks such as stale or duplicate registrations. worktree check becomes a thin alias for the worktree scope, keeping its handoff contract. Checks run in Go via endless-go; only --suggestions depends on the settings registry.
