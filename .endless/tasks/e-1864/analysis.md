Ships three verbs: 'decision unaccept' (accepted to proposed, guarded on 'accepted'), 'decision unreject' (rejected to proposed, guarded on 'rejected', and clears the stored rejection_reason), and 'decision reconsider' (dispatches to whichever applies, unguarded).

Deliberately NOT named 'reopen': a decision is proposed and then accepted or rejected, so it is never 'open'. The guarded pair is the primary surface because each verb asserts the status you believe you are in, so aiming one at the wrong terminal status errors instead of silently performing the other reversal.
