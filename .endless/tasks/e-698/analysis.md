Repetition and scheduling live in the trigger, not the runner — session monitor fires it on each refresh today; a future long-running daemon will fire it on events, including clock ticks. Concurrency-safe across the many monitors that may fire it simultaneously via a DB ticker + compare-and-set lease, so exactly one invocation claims and runs a given due job. Jobs are defined by their own tasks and register with the runner; the runner itself knows nothing job-specific.
