Found while landing E-2357 on 2026-11-07. The land fast-forwarded main, then its build of the installed endless-go failed with "updates to go.mod needed": endless's go.mod required go-dt v0.7.0 while go-cliutil v0.4.0, raised by E-2520, requires go-dt v0.7.1. The drift had sat on main since E-2520 landed, and every verify and land-time build in between passed.

Why nothing caught it: E-2563 writes a go.work into every Go task worktree, with `replace` directives pointing go-dt, go-cliutil and the other house modules at the local sibling checkouts of those modules. Under a go.work with those replaces, `go build` and `go test` never consult the stale requirement in go.mod, so a task's verify passes in its worktree while main, which has no go.work, cannot build. The failure only surfaces after the fast-forward, in the land's own build on main, which is the wrong moment: the merge is already done and the land is half-complete (no migration, no landing record) until someone fixes go.mod on main by hand.

The fix on main was one line, `go mod tidy`, but it had to be made in the main checkout outside any task, and the land of E-2357 then needed a fresh verify because the first land's rebase had moved the branch's SHA.

Shape of a fix: build the branch once with GOWORK=off (or against the module requirements as main will see them) before a verify can pass or a land can fast-forward — in `endless task verify`, in `worktree land --check` (E-2222), or in the land's pre-merge gates — so go.mod drift that the worktree's replaces hide fails the task in its own worktree, where the task can fix it, rather than main after the merge.
