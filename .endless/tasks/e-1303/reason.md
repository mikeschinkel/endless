Declined: it proposes a write the database forbids. The design was a prompt plus --release and --no-release flags to clear sessions.active_task_id after a land or a terminal status transition, so the binding could not outlive the worktree. Since then that column became write-once: set at claim, never cleared and never re-pointed, because it is the only route back to a session's transcript. A BEFORE UPDATE OF task_id trigger aborts any change including a clear, and 'endless task release' is now a tombstone that refuses and explains why. A stale binding is the accepted cost of that invariant, not a defect to flag. The land-time symptom it was filed for is handled where it belongs — in the caller, by checking whether the derived task still has a worktree (E-1304's territory).
