For a brainstorm the plan IS the deliverable, so 'no plan attached' can never mean 'needs a plan first' — a brainstorm whose problem statement is sufficient should route to submitted and be spawnable.

Make the routing type-aware: for brainstorm, judge whether the problem statement and constraints are sufficient to explore from, not whether a plan exists.

Check research tasks for the same mis-read, since their deliverable is the outcome rather than a plan.
