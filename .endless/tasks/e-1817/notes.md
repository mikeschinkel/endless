## Justification

The deliverable is a proposal for owner review, not a change. ED-1564 supplies the criterion (CLAUDE.md holds only self_dev, machine and owner-specific rules) but not the line-by-line calls, and deciding what leaves a file loaded into every session's context is judgment-heavy and owner-reviewed by design — the task text says so explicitly. The findings therefore cannot be inlined as a do-task: applying them without review is the failure mode the task was written to prevent.
