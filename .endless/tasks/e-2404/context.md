On 2026-10-18 05:35 UTC the tmux status line failed with "connecting to database ...: database is locked (261)" (SQLITE_BUSY_RECOVERY: another process was recovering the write-ahead log). Reproduced with modernc.org/sqlite v1.57.0: with another process holding the WAL write and recovery locks, a Ping fails at once with exactly "database is locked (261)"; with ?_pragma=busy_timeout(5000) in the DSN the same Ping waits and succeeds when the lock is released within 5s. Other sql.Open sites (internal/schema/open.go, backup, sandbox seeding) open their connection the same way.
