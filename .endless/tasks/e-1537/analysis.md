Epic-definition correction (2026-08): the definition must lead with CONTAINER-NESS, not size/duration. An epic is a container - never directly implemented, completion derives entirely from its children's states, no deliverable of its own; size and duration are NOT criteria (epics can be small). The 'multi-week narrative initiatives' phrasing in this description and in ED-1503 misleads by implying a size bar - it led a session to wrongly reject a small container as 'too small to be an epic.' When E-1545 writes the guide definition, lead with container-ness and omit 'multi-week' as a criterion. Scope note: 'completion derives from children' is defined for EPICS ONLY; non-epic parent-child completion semantics are unsettled.
