The minimizer, the judge and the challenger-generator are all sonnet/medium, so a candidate instruction is written, executed and graded by one model, and promotion is decided on that grade.

A scorer sharing the writer's blind spots prefers replies in its own idiom, so the loop can promote toward that idiom while reporting improvement — and nothing in the corpus can tell that from real quality, because only one opinion is recorded. The agent/minimizer pair is already independent; the scoring pair is not.
