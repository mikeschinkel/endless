Neither the recorder (internal/events/commit.go runGit) nor worktree land's managed-file commit (land_worktree Step 3) passes --no-verify, so every Endless ledger commit runs the project's pre-commit hook. In a Java or Rust project whose hook runs mvn verify or cargo clippy, that makes every recorded change slow and lets it fail whenever main does not build, which also refuses the land. Skipping hooks overrides a policy the team set, so this needs a decision rather than a patch. Not a concern until there are users with such hooks. Surfaced while planning E-2277 for PRODUCT usage.
