Superseded by E-1943/E-1941. The command's only purpose was making a branch current before the land built it; main's Step 4.2 (_rebuild_worktree_binary) now rebuilds the binary AFTER Step 4's rebase and before Step 5's ff-merge, so a stale binary cannot arise. The guard it existed to satisfy, _refuse_if_behind_base, was deleted outright along with BINARY_SOURCE_PATHS. Its implementation (commit 34dc226b) was discarded rather than merged: it references both now-gone symbols and would raise NameError on first use. It was also an auto-rebase of worktrees, which the original plan deliberately avoided and which is the operation that risks the ledger conflict E-1943 was opened to reason about. Removing the failure mode beat ordering around it.
