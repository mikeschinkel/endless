self_dev was asked for to mean 'this project is Endless'. What it documents itself as is narrower — 'this project's worktrees sandbox their endless.db writes' — and what it gates in practice is wider, including which binary a land uses for schema apply and whether internal/schema/changes/ is applied at all. Those are three different questions wearing one flag, and the gap between the name and the behaviour is the kind of leaky abstraction that costs hours of debugging when a future change assumes one meaning and gets another. It already caused a wrong conclusion in conversation on 2026-09-30, where the flag was read as the reason a land bug was Endless-only when the real reason was unrelated. Settle the use cases, then decide: keep the name, rename it, or split it.
