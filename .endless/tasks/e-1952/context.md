`task report` renders a report in one shot with no partial success and no persisted state, so any rejected or absent field costs a whole extra invocation —

one turn of an E-1939 session needed three calls to emit one block. A rejected note discards the valid `verify` field with it; the statelessness is undiscoverable given the id-addressed, payload-taking surface; a bare run at `unverified` asserts `Nothing to report.` for a task whose handoff mandates a verify command; and the PostToolUse hook fires on `--help`.
