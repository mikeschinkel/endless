The `handoff_verify` block in internal/templatecmd/templates/handoff/_close.tmpl tells every spawned session: "The suite goes at `.endless/tasks/e-<id>/verify.sh`, whose first statement sources the shared harness at `.endless/tasks/_harness.sh`". Its comment says it names `verify.sh` and not "or a verify.toml manifest" because verify.sh is the form a session writes by hand.

The verify.toml runner has landed (E-1602, E-1618), and manifests pass through `endless task verify` in practice: go-cfgstore's E-2206 and E-2208 suites are manifests. Most registered projects have no `_harness.sh` (go-cfgstore didn't), so a session that follows the handoff either cannot, or copies the harness in as part of an unrelated task, as happened in E-2208.
