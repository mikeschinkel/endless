The outcome IS the deliverable for research and brainstorm tasks, so --outcome must be supplied when completing one — keyed on type, not status. E-1240 had coupled the requirement to the 'completed' status as a proxy for deliverable-type; this expresses the intent directly. Epics never require an outcome (they self-complete via child-status derivation, with no interactive completion step). Decline keeps its own independent reason requirement (ED-1022/ED-1026, unchanged). Confirm and assume never require an outcome (already true in code; the guide table wrongly stated otherwise). Other types reaching 'completed' via a completable verb are not forced to carry an outcome; the completable-verb gate (ED-1511) governs who may reach 'completed', which this does not change.
