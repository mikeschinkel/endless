Glossary term. A code-bearing task is shipped once it has at least one landing record, whatever its status since. A research or brainstorm task, which never lands, is shipped when its outcome is accepted (completed). The session-status and project-status action with glyph ⏚ is labeled shipped and marks exactly this. Replaces the earlier use of shipped for the status group 'reached the verification gate or passed it', which is renamed reported. Rejected: implemented (says the work was written, not that it reached the primary branch); keeping both meanings of shipped.
