A worktree is 'settled' when its working tree is clean AND its branch is fully in main -- safe to reap. It is 'unsettled' when it is 'modified' (uncommitted working-tree changes) OR 'unlanded' (commits not in main). The reaper must never delete an unsettled worktree. This replaces the overloaded term 'dirty', which conflated the two sub-states: they need different fixes (commit-or-discard vs land) but share the do-not-reap rule.
