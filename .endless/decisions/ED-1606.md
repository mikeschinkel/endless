A session monitor does work only while its tmux window is visible (the active window of an attached client): a hidden monitor blocks idle, and recomputes once when it becomes visible. Visibility comes from tmux hooks, with a slow per-pane poll as the safety net that also checks the hooks are installed. Visible monitors fire the job runner; that describes the current trigger and does not rule out others. Rejected: a shared hub (deferred unless profiling after gating shows a need), an elected snapshot leader, cheaper-but-still-live panes, tmux control mode (an attached client corrupts the visibility signal), and a polling leader (needs a notify channel). Rationale in the E-2223 outcome.
