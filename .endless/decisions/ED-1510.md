Maybe-phase tasks must not be children of EPICS. Rationale: an epic's status auto-derives from its children (E-1541); a maybe (uncommitted) child creates phantom scope - the epic can appear done while an uncommitted commitment lingers inside, and auto-derivation cannot safely treat a maybe child as part of the epic's outcome. NON-epic parents do NOT auto-derive and their open children always surface for work (per the E-1863 relationship model), so a maybe child under a non-epic parent creates no phantom scope and is allowed. Therefore phase=maybe with an EPIC parent must not coexist; maybe under a non-epic parent is permitted. Enforcement at the write path (task add/update).
