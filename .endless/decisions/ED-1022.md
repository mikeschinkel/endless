When the resulting status is 'declined', outcome must be non-empty. Enforced at task decline (via Click required=True on --reason), task update --status declined (via _require_outcome_for_declined helper in update_plan), and task replace --status declined (via the same helper in replace_task). No back doors. Rationale: declining a task is uncommon and the reason is rarely obvious without explanation; forcing capture exactly there is where the cost/value math lands. Confirming, by contrast, usually means 'we tested it and it worked' — making outcome optional there avoids ceremonial logging.
