Add epic to authorized task types. An epic is a CONTAINER task: never directly implemented, and its completion derives entirely from its children's states - it has no deliverable of its own. Size and duration are NOT criteria (an epic can be small); what makes a task an epic is that it is complete only when its children are. Children may be known up front or emerge through planning. Status in_progress/completed auto-derive from child states; revisit/ready/declined/obsolete/blocked are explicit overrides. Terminal status is completed; no verify in v1 (maybe E-1535); all statuses reopenable. CLI aliases (endless epic) and pause-on-revisit hook are implementation details.
