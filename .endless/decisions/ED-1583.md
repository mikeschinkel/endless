A suite must be able to detect exactly one thing about running under the runner: that the real config and database are unreachable. Any other difference the isolation introduces is the runner bug. Found via the temp dir: it was reached through a symlink on macOS, so suites ran under a home directory no real machine has, and a test comparing a resolved path against the home dir failed inside suites that had nothing to do with the change being verified. E-2094 proposed fixing the test; we fixed the runner. Stated without naming a mechanism on purpose, because the current one (replacing HOME and XDG_CONFIG_HOME) is being deprecated by E-1964: this binds whatever replaces it, and the Tier 1+ substrate ladder, not just today implementation. A leak makes verdicts depend on the OS and blames the wrong task.
