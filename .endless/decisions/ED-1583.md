The runner swaps HOME and XDG_CONFIG_HOME for a temp dir. On macOS that dir is reached through a symlink, so every suite ran under a HOME no real machine has — and a test comparing a resolved path against os.UserHomeDir failed inside suites that had nothing to do with the change being verified. E-2094 proposed fixing the test; we fixed the runner instead. The rule: anything the isolation changes, other than hiding the real config and database, is the runner bug. It is not a property of the code under test, it makes a verdict depend on the OS, and it blames the wrong task. Applies to the Tier 1+ substrate ladder when it lands.
