Renames the --as flag on 'endless task link' and 'endless task unlink' to --type. Rationale: agents reach for --type because it is the convention across most CLIs; --as reads nicely in English ('link X to Y as blocks') but loses discoverability and forces a remembering step. Per the CLI-naming-weights-agent-reach principle (memory:feedback_cli_naming_agent_friction), friction at the flag-name level compounds across thousands of agent invocations. No backward compatibility because endless is pre-1.0 and has no production users; --as is removed entirely. E-1139's planned 'endless decision link' will adopt --type from inception. Note: I (Claude in another session) chose --as originally; user disliked it but had no better alternative until --type surfaced as the cowpath.
