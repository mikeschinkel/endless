An agent takes the first sanctioned option a refusal offers and stops reading for alternatives, so remedy ORDER is behavior, not prose style. Measured twice: the path gate put --allow-path in the last clause of the last sentence, behind 'put real content inline', and a session that read the whole message still rephrased its content to satisfy the gate — distorting the very content the gate exists to protect. Rule: when a refusal has an escape hatch that keeps the caller's content intact, that hatch leads. A remedy that alters, truncates or re-authors content comes after it, and rationale comes last, where it cannot be mistaken for an instruction. Corollary: never offer a remedy the field cannot accept (a single-line capped field was told to load itself from a file, earning a second refusal). Applies to every refusal Endless emits to an agent, not just the path gate.
