Write-once invariants for nullable columns are enforced at the DB level via BEFORE UPDATE triggers (RAISE(ABORT, ...) when OLD is non-NULL and NEW differs). CHECK constraints remain prohibited project-wide per E-892's catastrophic-data-loss lesson: SQLite cannot ALTER/DROP them without rebuilding the table. Triggers do not share that limitation; they can be DROP TRIGGERed cleanly. Application-layer pre-checks are additive for friendly error messages; the trigger is the authoritative gate.
