How a branch lands is declared in .endless/tasks/e-<id>/land.toml on that branch, not in a task field or a generic task.toml: it travels and rebases with the code, shows in the reviewed diff, and is what the land reads. Keys always sit in tables; Endless-internal keys go under [self_dev]; unknown keys refuse the land before the merge. A project-wide .endless/land.toml overridden per task is the intended layering. Chosen over a land flag, a DB field and a catch-all task.toml (Mike, 2026-09-29).
