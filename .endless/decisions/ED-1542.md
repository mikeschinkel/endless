A decision is proposed and then accepted or rejected — it is never 'open', so 'reopen' (the task-tree verb) is a category error when applied to decisions. E-1864 ships 'decision unaccept' and 'decision unreject' as the primary surface: each names the status it undoes and refuses if the decision is not in that status, so aiming one at the wrong terminal status errors instead of silently performing the other reversal. 'decision reconsider' is the unguarded convenience for when you do not care which way the decision went. Reversing is for correcting the record (an accidental accept, a reject you want to re-argue); overturning a decision that was rightly settled is still better captured as a new decision linked with --type reverses.
