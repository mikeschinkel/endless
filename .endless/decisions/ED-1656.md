event emit appends the ledger line after the apply's SQL and before the COMMIT, in one BEGIN IMMEDIATE transaction, so a SQL failure leaves nothing on the ledger. When the COMMIT fails after the append, a ledger.voided entry names the lines by segment and byte offset and replay skips them; endless-go event void writes one by hand, user-only. This keeps the purpose of E-808's 'write the event first' (no committed change without its record) and drops its letter, since E-2062 made the rebuild that would have applied an orphaned line refuse.
