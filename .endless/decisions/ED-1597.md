Endless is moving many subcommands to top-level commands. Filing a new verb under a parent in the meantime buys nothing and schedules a rename: the parent is provisional, so the nesting encodes a grouping that is already known to be temporary, and every consumer — muscle memory, docs, guide text, scripts — pays the move twice. Choose the name once, at top level, and let the migration bring the rest up to it rather than adding to what has to be migrated. Raised while deciding where a 'touch' verb belongs: 'why decide parent and later move to top-level?' Applies to new verbs only; it does not by itself relocate an existing subcommand, which is the migration's own work.
