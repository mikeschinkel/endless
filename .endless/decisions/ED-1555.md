Per the Bitter Lesson, a hand-picked label set is the hand-crafted feature to avoid. Fix the MECHANISM — $TOKEN "quoted span" — and leave the VOCABULARY open: the user writes what is natural, and the taxonomy is discovered from what they actually type. Span-scoping is the substantive half: turn-level labels train almost nothing, span-level trains a lot. Consistency is LEANED ON, not enforced — when a new token clusters near one used before, the loop asks in-band whether to merge, and the user may answer or ignore. Accepting short-term scoring noise is the right trade: a vocabulary the user cannot recall in the moment produces no label at all, and label supply is upstream of everything.
