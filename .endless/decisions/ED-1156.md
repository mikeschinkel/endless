Establishes typed relationships for decision pairs. Canonical vocabulary locks to two stored types: 'reverses' (prior decision no longer in effect; this one takes its place — subsumes overturns/rescinds/revokes/annuls/overrules/vacates/abrogates/countermands/sets_aside/quashes per E-1003's narrow-and-reachable principle) and 'modifies' (changes part of prior; prior partially in effect). Each gets an inverse display name (reversed_by, modified_by). 'clarifies' and 'reaffirms' deferred to a maybe-phase task per YAGNI; promotable when concrete need surfaces. Schema reuses task_deps (decisions are unified with tasks; column permissive per E-958, no migration). New 'endless decision link' and 'endless decision unlink' commands are thin wrappers with both-args-are-decisions validation; 'endless task link' continues to accept any IDs unchanged. Retypes E-1140 → E-1112 (documents → reverses) and E-1141 → E-1056 (documents → modifies) once shipped.
