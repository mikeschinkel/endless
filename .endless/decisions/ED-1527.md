The append-only invariant protects legitimate history, not every byte ever appended. Three classes of ledger line, three treatments: (1) Legitimate events are immutable forever; corrections happen by appending new events. (2) Real-but-wrong events — genuine actions recorded with wrong content — are neutralized by appended tombstone/revert entries the projector skips (the E-1716/E-1720 mechanism); mandatory once segments sync across nodes, because another node may already have consumed them. (3) Bug-written garbage — lines a defect wrote into a ledger they were never legitimate history of — may be excised by editing the segment files in a normal hygiene commit; the git history of the mistake is preserved, the ledger's meaning is not rewritten because these lines never carried meaning. Classification between (2) and (3) is per-line, never per-node: node ids can be shared or interleave real events with garbage.
