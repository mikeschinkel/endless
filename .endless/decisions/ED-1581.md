The idle->working session wake was first put inside monitor.TouchSession, on the reasoning that it is the one place every turn reaches. It is also reached by callers that are NOT the session: EnsureClaudeSessionID resolves a Claude session's row on behalf of a sibling SHELL pane, and that caller has observed nothing about whether the session is acting — so the wake fired on every command a human ran in a spawn window's shell pane and on every monitor repaint, and an idle session would have read as 'working' forever. The rule: a shared touch/upsert helper records PRESENCE and stays state-preserving; a transition whose meaning is an observation is written by the observer, as its own verb beside it. sessions.state has five writers, so this binds the next one. Sibling to ED-1579 — that governs what a check may believe, this governs who may assert a write.
