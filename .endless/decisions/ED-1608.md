A task's description, context, analysis, plan, outcome and notes are published, as are decisions, lessons and commit messages. None of them may cite a private or gitignored document — not by path, not by filename, not by description — because the citation leaks the document's existence and subject, and because the path resolves for nobody else, making the published text incomplete by construction. Such content must be self-contained with respect to private material: it carries the substance it needs, or cites a committed path. Before citing, check whether the path is committed; a git-ignored path or anything under a private directory is not citable. Rejected: relocating the private document wholesale to a public path, which is not automatically safe either, since the material may carry its own publication posture that moving it would violate.
