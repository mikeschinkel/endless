Tasks kept together only for tracking, rather than decomposed from a strategy, belong under a task of type group, not epic, so each type can have its own rules, gates and handoffs. A group needs nothing beyond its title and description: its completion derives from its children and its membership is clear from its title. Neither contains the other: a group holds no epics and an epic holds no groups, so a task cannot sit in a group and under an initiative at once, and that is accepted. The type is chosen at filing, when it should usually be clear whether the work is an initiative or a convenience grouping; a group may be changed to an epic when someone realizes it is one, which should be rare. A group can be claimed and spawned, and its session only coordinates.
