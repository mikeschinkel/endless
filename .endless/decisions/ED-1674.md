Backlog counts tasks that are not in a terminal status (confirmed, assumed, completed, declined, obsolete, superseded). Total Backlog, shown on the console's tmux status line, is the sum of all such tasks. project show reports four further measures over the same tasks: Unspawned Backlog (not yet spawned), Spawned Backlog (have been spawned), Backlog by status, and Backlog by phase. Rejected: counting only tasks nobody has started (unplanned, submitted, ready), which hides work in flight. This definition stands until a glossary carries the term. Mike, planning E-2652.
