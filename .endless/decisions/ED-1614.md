A fault (ERR/WARN) is for an exceptional condition a person must act on. A condition that will come up in the normal course of use — a retained worktree left behind by a rewrite, a network blip a retry will absorb — goes to a state surface (a job's run note, a status view, a check) instead, or reaches the error list only once it stops being routine (a threshold, a self-clearing incident).
