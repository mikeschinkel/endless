type=bug tasks represent the defect and use the generic task lifecycle. When diagnosis is nontrivial, create a research child task: its 'text' is the investigation approach, its 'outcome' is the findings. The bug task's 'text' holds the fix plan (how to fix); the bug is what moves through underway -> unverified -> confirmed once fixed. Trivial bugs need no research child. This avoids (a) misusing the bug's 'text' field for findings and (b) forcing 'investigate' and 'fix' to be statuses — they are activities/phases. Prompted by E-1619, where investigation results had been written into the bug's 'text'.
