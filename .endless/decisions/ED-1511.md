The verb-completability gate (ED-1502 / E-1240) exists so completed is reserved for findings-as-deliverable tasks (audits, research, reviews) where the outcome text IS the deliverable. Epics fit that definition: an epic's deliverable IS the outcome (a coordination summary of what shipped in its children), not behavior. The verb gate was scoped at imperative implementation tasks where behavior is the deliverable; applying it to epics is over-reach. Concrete deadlock observed on E-1564 (type=epic + lead verb 'implement') where the type gate forces completed but the verb gate rejects it. Therefore: when tasks.type='epic', skip the verb gate; the type gate alone is correct enforcement.
