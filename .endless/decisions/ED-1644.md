No go.mod may require or replace github.com/mikeschinkel/go-doterr, except the go-doterr repo's own test/, examples/ and cmd/ modules. A package uses doterr by holding its own copy of doterr.go, synced from go-doterr (`make sync DIR=<path>` in the go-doterr checkout), so its calls need no package qualifier. go-doterr's doterr.go changes only with the owner's explicit approval.
