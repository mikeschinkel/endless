brainstorm is the requester-led sibling of research. research pulls information IN from external sources, agent-driven; brainstorm draws it OUT of the requester via interview, requester-driven. Both produce information-shaped artifacts (text = seed/request, outcome = synthesis + spawned follow-ups), but the dividing line is direction of information flow. The type exists to attach a behavioral contract: open by interviewing the requester, do NOT research autonomously or jump to a plan, surface tensions and options, capture ideas, conclude with synthesis and spawned tasks/decisions. Ungated — unlike research (ED-1504), no justification is required, because frictionless ideation is desirable. Spawn handoff branches on type so a spawned brainstorm opens in interview mode (a brainstorm as an epic's first child is a primary use case).
