Each endless-managed worktree keeps two separate files under its .endless/: worktree.json holds write-once IDENTITY (kind, base_branch, branch, created_at) — set at creation, never changed for the worktree's life; worktree.lock holds write-often OWNERSHIP (session_id, pid, tmux_pane, claimed_at) — written on claim, deleted on release, rewritten by the next session. They are kept separate BECAUSE their lifecycles differ: releasing a worktree removes the lock (an rm), which must never touch or risk corrupting the durable identity, and a crash mid-lock-write cannot damage identity; the identity persists across sessions while the lock churns per session. Merging them would couple ephemeral ownership churn to write-once identity — an anti-goal. This ADR documents existing shipped design (the E-971 worktree foundation); no code change. Written after a session mistook the two files for a mergeable redundancy.
