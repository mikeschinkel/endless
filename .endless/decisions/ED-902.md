Kairos timestamps are 75-bit values (52-bit microsecond offset from 2000-01-01, 7-bit logical counter, 16-bit node ID) encoded as 15 chars of Crockford Base32. Epoch 2000-01-01 covers until 2142 and predates any realistic JIRA/task history. Fixed epoch, not configurable, because different epochs between machines would break cross-machine timestamp interpretation. Decided 2026-04-25.
